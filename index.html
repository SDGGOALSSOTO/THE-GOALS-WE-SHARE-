Why it matters

Progress in one place
can lift many others.

A child who can learn, a family with reliable clean water, or a city with safer streets all show how sustainability touches daily life. Because the goals are linked, solutions work best when they improve people’s lives while caring for the planet.

01 · Dignity

More people can thrive

Health, education, food and decent work give people more freedom to shape their own futures and take part in their communities.

02 · Resilience

Communities can weather change

Clean energy, strong public services and protected ecosystems help communities prepare for shocks and recover together.

03 · Belonging

Fairer systems include more voices

Reducing discrimination and strengthening justice helps people feel safe, be heard and access opportunities on fair terms.

Values in practice

What kind of society do we want?

The 2030 Agenda puts human dignity, equality, rights and the promise to leave no one behind at its heart. These shared values help us ask whether progress is reaching everyone and lasting for generations.

The value connections on each goal are an educational interpretation grounded in the Agenda’s principles.

DignityEveryone deserves the basics and the chance to live with respect.
FairnessRights and opportunities should not depend on identity or background.
CareWe protect people, living systems and those who come after us.
ResponsibilityChoices today should account for their wider and longer-term effects.
ParticipationPeople affected by decisions should have a voice in shaping them.
SolidarityWe make shared progress by supporting one another across borders.
